Privacy Policy

Foursan Al Adala

Last Updated: 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Foursan Al Adala (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles user information and personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal data such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • National ID
  • Date of birth
  • Exact location
  • Personal photos
  • Personal files or documents
  • Account credentials
  • Email address unless provided voluntarily for support

The app is designed to operate without creating user accounts and without collecting personal information from users directly.

2. Local Data Use Within the Device

The app may store limited information locally on the user’s device only to support its functionality, such as:

  • App settings
  • Local activation data
  • Cached files
  • Document files selected by the user
  • Temporary local records created while using the app

This data remains on the device and is used only for the app’s local operation. It is not shared with third parties or transferred to external servers by us.

3. Permissions

The app may request access to device features when needed for its functions, such as:

  • Camera access for document capture or image scanning
  • Media or file access for selecting and viewing documents
  • Audio recording for voice-related features
  • Storage access to save or open local files
  • Internet access for external support links or online resources

These permissions are used only to provide the intended app features and are not used to collect personal data for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data with third parties.

We do not use third-party analytics tools that track personal user behavior.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect local device data used by the app. Since personal data is not collected centrally, the main data storage is limited to the user’s own device.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. External Links

The app may contain links to external websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. Please review their privacy policies separately.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

support@foursan-al-adala.com

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the updated date will be revised accordingly.
